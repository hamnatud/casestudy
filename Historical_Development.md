# Nokia's Introduction to phones 
Nokia's first venture into the mobile phone business was in 1979 as a joint venture with Salora Oy a Finnish company that does electronics, they established Mobira as a joint venture to make their radio telephones. 
Mobira was the first to launch an international cellular network with the possibility to allow international roaming, they called this the Nordic Mobile Telephone and it was launched in 1981. 
They then introduced the first car phone in 1982 which introduced the companies first digital telephone switch.
The companies first major milestone for phones came in 1987 with their first compact phone they called “Mobira Cityman 900” which was the first handheld cell phone. 
In 1992  the next CEO Jorma Ollila changed the Nokia business towards being a telecom-oriented company after axing a few industrial units like their power business. This turned out  to be a great strategy as the company proceeded to rapidly grow in the coming years. 
In 1992 Nokia broke another major milestone with their new Nokia 1011 phone. This phone was capable of receiving messages only and receiving and making calls.
In 1996 they released the Nokia 9000 communicator which was their “all in one” phone, allowing people to send emails, fax and browse the web on it.
Another major milestone in phone technology was the introduction of cameras. The phone they created was called the Nokia 7650 and they launched it in 2001. The phone was also a first in full color display which is another milestone in itself. 
In 2002 they introduced the first phone with 3G (3G meaning third generation) called the Nokia 6650. They also released their first phone with a video recorder the same year called the Nokia 3650 
# The introduction of Iphones
The first iphone was marketed as a phone that could be used as an ipod, phone and had the use of the internet by Steve Jobs the co-founder of Apple. This phone also took away the keyboard that every phone had before it and embraced full touch screen which was revolutionary for the phone technologies. 
In 2008 they released their iphone 3G which introduced faster 3G connectivity and introduced the apple store to the iphone which was a major milestone for developers who wanted to create applications.
in 2009 they released their iphone 3GS which introduced the video recorder to their phones.
In 2010 the Iphone 4  came out and added a new HD video camera which was the first phone to allow for HD video capture, the camera also had a LED flash light. This iphones major new function was facetime which allowed for two users to video call each other. This was Apple’s second major milestone for improving the technology. 
In 2011 the debut of Siri the virtual assistant was introduced on the iphone 4S. The iphone 4S also came with a 1080p video camera which yet again improved on their camera technology for their iphones.
In 2012 the iphone 5s was the first smartphone that had a 64 bit processor and also had a touch ID feature to unlock your phone with.
The iphone 6S came out with its 3D touch technology and its very impressive 4K video camera. The 3D touch allowed for the device to measure how much pressure you are applying to the screen which allowed the smartphone to run different commands based off the pressure. 
In 2017, the iPhone X departed from the classic iPhone by removing the home button and making the whole screen touch screen and added a face ID technology. 
In 2021, the iPhone 13 improved battery life and camera quality, including better automatic focusing. 
In 2022, the iPhone 14 introduced Emergency SOS, which allowed users to contact emergency services through satellite communication in supported areas. 
Apple continues to release new iPhones every year, although recent models have mainly focused on improving existing features rather than introducing completely new technology.
